Dragonoa Online: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This policy explains what information the game Dragonoa Online (Android package com.dragonoa.online) collects, why, and what you can do about it. The game is made by an independent developer in the Netherlands. Questions and requests: okimuru@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your account: the username and email address you register with, and your password. The password is stored only in hashed form.
  • Your game data: your characters and their names, level, items, gold, gems, quests, progress and rankings, and the social parts of the game: party, guild, friends, blocked players, mail and trades with other players.
  • Chat: messages you send in Global, Party, Guild and Whispers are delivered by our server to the players they are meant for. The server keeps each player's last messages in memory for up to 30 minutes so that abuse can be reported, and shows the last minute of Global and Guild chat to players who log in. Chat is not saved otherwise. When a player reports another player, the report stores the reported player's recent messages that the reporting player received, so a moderator can check it.
  • Technical data: the IP address your phone connects from (needed to connect, and written in our server logs), the game version, and while you play, the frame rate and phone temperature every 30 seconds. After a dropped connection the game sends the warnings and errors it saw just before, to help us fix it.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, files or payment details. The game has no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.

Why we use it

  • To run the game and your account (this is the service you ask for).
  • To keep the game fair and safe: to stop cheating, spam and abuse, and to handle reports.
  • To find and fix crashes, lag and other problems.

Under the GDPR we rely on the contract with you (providing the game) and on our legitimate interest in a safe, working game.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your data and do not share it with advertisers. Your data is stored on servers run for us by our hosting provider in the European Union. Other players see what the game shows them: your character's name, appearance, level and your chat messages. We disclose data only if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Account and game data: as long as your account exists.
  • Chat: up to 30 minutes in server memory, as described above. Reports and the messages saved with them: up to 12 months.
  • Server logs: for a limited time, normally no longer than 30 days.
  • Backups of our databases are kept for up to 30 days, after which deleted data is gone from them too.

Deleting your account

In the game: open Settings (the gear, top right), tap Delete account and type DELETE. Your login stops working at once and your account and all its characters are deleted within minutes. If you cannot log in, see Delete your Dragonoa Online account.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it or to stop a use you object to. Write to okimuru@gmail.com from the email address of your account. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in the Netherlands: the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Children

Dragonoa Online is not meant for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Passwords are stored hashed and access to our servers is restricted. The game's connection is not encrypted yet, so please do not use a password that you also use somewhere else.

Changes

When this policy changes we update the date at the top of this page.
